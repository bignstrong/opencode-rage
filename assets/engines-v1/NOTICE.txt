Rage managed engines, engines-v1

These ten gzip archives are unchanged copies from ihxnnxs/opencode-voice
v0.2.0. Rage replaced only download URLs in registry.json. Keep
ENGINE-LICENSES.zip with every distribution of these engines. It contains
the upstream license texts and copyright notices, not just license names.

Build: https://github.com/ihxnnxs/opencode-voice/actions/runs/31380639622
Recipe and sidecar lockfile: ihxnnxs/opencode-voice at
0938e1a34fd09e7ce4497a18032607f4b346613b.

Contents of ENGINE-LICENSES.zip:

- opencode-voice: original MIT license for the sidecar and release metadata.
- whisper.cpp: ggml-org/whisper.cpp v1.8.2 at
  4979e04f5dcaccb36057e059bbaed8a2f5288315, including the license
  blocks from the compiled miniaudio and stb_vorbis sources.
- ggml-components: notices for the Mozilla llamafile kernels, YaRN,
  Arm optimized math routines, and FP16 code referenced by ggml.
- transcribe.cpp: actual THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.md from handy-computer/transcribe.cpp
  at a94e021ef658dc7c788837341a13f6acea3baf3c, covering the vendored
  ggml and miniz libraries; also the llama.cpp and Unicode data notices
  for the tokenizer. The Rust bindings and sys crate carry MIT licenses.
- crates: license files from all crates in the sidecar's release Cargo.lock,
  including build dependencies. Each .crate matched its Cargo.lock SHA-256.
  Hound is Apache-2.0, Copyright (C) 2015 Ruud van Asseldonk.
- rust: the Rust 1.97.1 standard library copyright inventory and license
  texts from its official toolchain distribution. All five sidecars identify
  rustc commit 8bab26f4f68e0e26f0bb7960be334d5b520ea452.

These releases contain no models, FFmpeg, Windows CRT DLLs or Apple frameworks.
